# Drift And Drive Showroom

I want you to listen very carefully to what I'm about to explain.

I want to design a **car rental website** with an almost minimalistic yet stylish appearance. The design should have a 3D feel. By 3D, I don't necessarily mean actual 3D models everywhere; I mean scroll-based animations and interactive effects, particularly using GSAP animation techniques.

I already have a few concepts and some empty project files that I want to develop. I'll show them to you shortly.

But before that, I want to understand how you would approach and start building this kind of website. Then I'll provide you with the complete brief.

One thing I absolutely don't want is generic AI slop. The design must be creative, intentional, and professionally crafted.

### 1. Overall Website Concept

The website should have a simple, clean design with a car prominently displayed in the hero section.

We can choose any suitable car for the initial design. The car should be positioned prominently, preferably facing the viewer.

The main idea is to create an interactive experience where the car itself becomes part of the website's animations.

### 2. Scroll-Based Car Animation

When someone starts scrolling, the car should move along with the scrolling experience.

The car should gradually move away from its initial position, creating the feeling that it is actually driving through the website.

I also want a **smoke animation effect**, similar to the drifting and turning effects seen in the game **Need for Speed Unbound**.

Whenever the car moves or turns during scrolling, smoke should appear around its tyres, creating a similar visual experience.

This effect should continue naturally throughout the website rather than looking like a separate animation placed inside an isolated container.

### 3. Interactive Booking Experience

The entire experience should be designed around the car.

For example, as the visitor scrolls through the website, the car should gradually move through the different sections.

Within approximately three to four scrolls or swipes, the car should complete its movement through the hero experience and reach the booking section.

At that point, the user should be able to interact with the booking interface.

The booking system should include:

- **Location:** The service will operate exclusively within Lucknow. There is no need for users to select a location.
- **Pickup Date:** Select the date from which the vehicle is required.
- **Return Date:** Select the date until which the vehicle is required.
- **Vehicle Selection:** Choose the preferred car or vehicle.
- **Booking:** Proceed with the booking process.

### 4. Car-Themed Interactions and Animations

All animations and interactions throughout the website should follow the automotive theme.

For example, when someone clicks a button, instead of displaying a generic loading animation or transition, we could introduce a tyre-spinning animation or another car-inspired interaction.

Every transition should feel connected to the overall driving experience.

The idea is to make the entire website feel like an interactive automotive experience rather than a conventional car rental website.

**The overall direction should be minimal, stylish, modern, and highly interactive, with GSAP-powered scroll animations, realistic car movement, drifting smoke effects, and a seamless booking experience.**
